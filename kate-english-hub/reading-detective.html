Reflection prompts

60-minute teaching sequence

  1. 0–8 min — retrieve old errors / warm-up
  2. 8–18 min — paraphrase drill, 10 items
  3. 18–37 min — silent reading & 7 questions
  4. 37–52 min — defend answers with text evidence
  5. 52–60 min — exit ticket & error log
