Task 1 · Rules

max 2
2Easy to follow, no unnatural pauses. Up to 5 phonetic mistakes, of them only 1–2 change the meaning.
1Some unnatural pauses. Up to 7 phonetic mistakes, of them 3 change the meaning.
0Hard to follow, or more than 7 mistakes, or 4+ mistakes that change the meaning.
  • Meaning-changing: ship → sheep, put read as cut.
  • Every missed or added word (a, the, in, and) is a serious mistake. Four of them = 0.
  • 2–3 words left unread — the task is still checked. More than 3 words or a whole line missed = 0.
  • Watch: numbers and years, stress in long words, silent letters (castle), lists.
  • Intonation: falling in statements and wh-questions, rising in yes/no questions and in lists.

Task 2 · Rules

6 × 1 point
1A full answer to the question. Small mistakes are OK if they don't stop understanding.
0No answer · off the question · one word or a phrase · “I don't know / I don't remember” · a factual mistake · mistakes that block understanding.
  • You hear each question once. Think, then speak: 40 seconds is enough.
  • A short answer + a full sentence counts: “In Moscow. I live in Moscow.”
  • If you correct yourself, only your last version is scored.
  • Keep the tense of the question: Did…? → past · Would you like…? → I'd like…
  • Why? / In what way? → give a reason. What is he like? = character, not looks.
  • A missing -s (he like) does not cost the point in the OGE.

0No opening and no closing, and/or 4+ linking mistakes
2Up to 4 minor grammar/vocabulary mistakes and up to 3 phonetic
1Up to 5 grammar/vocabulary and up to 4 phonetic
06+ grammar/vocabulary or 5+ phonetic, or more than 3 serious mistakes
  • Opening and closing both name the topic — in English.
  • Every plan point, in order; if it says why, give a reason.
  • Linkers between all parts. No extra information outside the plan.
  • Count your phrases in practice; stop before 2 minutes.
