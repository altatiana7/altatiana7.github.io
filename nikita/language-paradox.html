06 · GRAMMAR & DEBATE · 12 MIN

Mixed Conditionals — the Language of Hypotheses

Today's debate lives in conditionals. Brush up the machinery, practise on the lesson's own questions, then argue.
📐 Reference — the three patterns (open if needed)
PatternFormToday's example
2nd — unreal presentIf + past simple → would/could/might + VIf the world had one language, we would lose the music of 7,000 voices.
3rd — unreal pastIf + had + V3 → would have + V3If we hadn't invented writing, history would have developed completely differently.
Mixed — past cause → present result (and vice versa)If + had + V3 → would + V  /  If + past simple → would have + V3If Esperanto had caught on in 1887, English wouldn't be so dominant now. If Nikita weren't good at languages, he wouldn't have started Arabic news so soon.

Exercise 1 · Choose the machine

Pick the correct forms — then justify: which pattern is it?

Exercise 2 · Finish the hypothesis

Complete each one YOUR way — one clause is enough. Then compare with the sample ideas.

Debate toolkit: “I'd push back on that…” · “That's fair, but consider…” · “Admittedly…, however…” · “It depends on what we mean by…” · “The evidence from the video suggests the opposite…”
